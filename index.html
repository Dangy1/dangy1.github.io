Yongchao Dang
yongchao [dot] dang [at] aalto [dot] fi  ·  dangyc1992 [at] gmail [dot] com

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Information and Communications Engineering at Aalto University, Finland, working with Prof. Riku Jäntti. In spring 2025 I was a visiting scientist at EURECOM, Sophia Antipolis, France.

My research centers on cellular-connected UAVs and AI/ML for wireless networks and O-RAN. I work on GPS spoofing detection and mitigation for cellular-connected systems, agentic AI/ML for network optimization, automation and security analytics, and wireless time-sensitive networking over Wi-Fi, DECT-2020 NR, 5G and 6G. I also work on physical layer security and authentication, including low-energy backscatter communication. Much of this is hands-on: I have implemented ETSI DECT-2020 NR on Nordic Semiconductor hardware, built a radio intelligent controller on OpenWiFi for wireless TSN and Industry 5.0, and deployed O-RAN on OAI and srsRAN testbeds.

I received my Doctor of Science (Technology) from Aalto University in 2023 with a thesis on ML-based GPS spoofing detection and mitigation for cellular-connected UAVs. Before that, I earned an M.S. in Computer Science and Technology from Xidian University (2019), including an exchange year at Future University Hakodate, Japan, and a B.S. from Northwestern Polytechnical University (2016). I have contributed to EU Horizon 2020 flagship projects such as 5G!Drones, INSPIRE-5Gplus and TERAWAY, and to Finnish national projects including Cloudify-6G and 6G-eMTC. I received the Nokia Scholarship (2022) and the IEEE GLOBECOM Best Paper Award (2020).

Research Projects
  • AURORA-6G: AI-driven unified O-RAN for real-time autonomous zero-touch networks (2025-).
  • 6GARROW: 6G AI-native integrated RAN-core networks. EU SNS, Finland and Republic of Korea joint project (2025-2027). CU-UP integration with the core UPF for edge-based URLLC; architecture review and design of the integrated RAN-core solution.
  • Cloudify-6G: Data-driven distributed cloud and communication system. Business Finland (2023-2026). Use-case analysis, radio protocol design, and platform development on IS-Wireless O-RAN, OAI FlexRIC and srsRAN RIC; developed near-real-time KPM and xApps for RAN slicing and resource scheduling.
  • 6G-eMTC: Extreme machine-type communications for 6G. Business Finland (2023-2024). DECT-2020 NR MAC layer implementation on the Nordic nRF9161.
  • 5G-TSN: Wireless TSN over a programmable UPF, visiting EURECOM (2025). Mapping TSN QoS to 5G QFI with reinforcement learning and other methods.
  • Drolo-I / Drolo-II: GPS spoofing detection and mitigation. EU Recovery and Resilience Facility (2023-2025). Spoofing-resistant positioning and network measurements of cellular-connected UAVs.
  • INSPIRE-5Gplus: Intelligent security and pervasive trust for 5G and beyond. EU Horizon 2020 (2019-2022). UAV position monitoring and anti-GPS-spoofing enabler; deliverables D2.1 and D3.1 on 5G security.
  • TERAWAY: Terahertz technology for ultra-broadband operation inside 5G networks with SDN management. EU Horizon 2020 (2019-2023). Radio map construction, deterministic channel modelling and ray tracing tool design.
  • 5G!Drones: UAV vertical application trials leveraging advanced 5G facilities. EU Horizon 2020 (2019-2022). Designed and developed a 5G-connected UAV system on Aalto's 5G network.
