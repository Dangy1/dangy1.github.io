Yongchao Dang

Postdoctoral Researcher · Aalto University, Finland

I work on wireless networks and AI with Prof. Riku Jäntti at the Department of Information and Communications Engineering. My current focus is agentic AI for automating O-RAN and 6G networks, GPS spoofing detection for cellular-connected UAVs, and secure, deterministic connectivity over DECT-2020 NR, Wi-Fi and 5G.

I hold a D.Sc. (Tech.) from Aalto University (2023) and an M.S. from Xidian University (2019). I received the Nokia Scholarship in 2022 and the IEEE GLOBECOM Best Paper Award in 2020.

Selected Publications

Representative papers are shaded. Full list below and on Google Scholar.

Physical Layer Challenge-Response Authentication Between Ambient Backscatter Devices
Yifan Zhang, Yongchao Dang, Masoud Kaveh, Zheng Yan, Riku Jäntti, Zhu Han
IEEE Transactions on Information Forensics and Security, 2026
paperarXiv

A lightweight challenge-response authentication scheme that runs entirely at the physical layer between battery-free ambient backscatter devices.

AppWeaver: Agentic Discovery and Composition of O-RAN App Workflows for 6G
Yongchao Dang, Tao Chen, Riku Jäntti
Submitted to IEEE Wireless Communications, 2026

An agentic AI framework that discovers, selects and composes O-RAN xApps and rApps into end-to-end workflows for 6G network automation.

Uni-RAT: A Unified DECT-2020 NR and Wi-Fi System for 6G Multi-RAT Networks
Yongchao Dang, Quang Ngo, Kalle Ruttik, Jose Costa-Requena, Riku Jäntti
Submitted to Computer Networks, 2026

Integrates DECT-2020 NR as a non-3GPP access into a multi-RAT architecture alongside Wi-Fi, with a prototype on Nordic hardware and open-source 5G core.

EWDC: Integrating DECT-2020 With Wi-Fi for Enhanced Wireless Direct Connectivity
Yongchao Dang, Quang Ngo, Roni Fagerholm, Veli-Matti Rantanen, Kalle Ruttik, Riku Jäntti
IEEE Internet of Things Journal, 2025
paperpreprint

Replaces Bluetooth LE with DECT-2020 NR for secure device discovery and pairing, then hands off to Wi-Fi for high-speed transfer. Implemented on the Nordic nRF9161.

Open Radio Intelligent Controller Based Wireless Time Sensitive Networking for Industry 5.0
Yongchao Dang, Roni Fagerholm, Kalle Ruttik, Tao Chen, Riku Jäntti
Authorea / TechRxiv preprint, 2025
preprint

A radio intelligent controller built on OpenWiFi that brings time-sensitive networking guarantees to wireless links for industrial use.

3D Radio Map-Based GPS Spoofing Detection and Mitigation for Cellular-Connected UAVs
Yongchao Dang, Alp Karakoc, Saba Norshahida, Riku Jäntti
IEEE Transactions on Machine Learning in Communications and Networking, vol. 1, pp. 313-327, 2023
paperpdfvideo

Builds a fine-grained 3D radio map with ray tracing and Kriging, detects spoofing by comparing measured and predicted RSS with ML, and relocates the UAV with a particle filter to within 10 m.

Deep-Ensemble-Learning-Based GPS Spoofing Detection for Cellular-Connected UAVs
Yongchao Dang, Chafika Benzaïd, Bin Yang, Tarik Taleb, Yulong Shen
IEEE Internet of Things Journal, vol. 9, no. 24, pp. 25068-25085, 2022
paperpdf

Detects spoofed trajectories at the mobile edge from path-loss measurements alone, with above 97% accuracy using two base stations and no extra UAV hardware.

GPS Spoofing Detector with Adaptive Trustable Residence Area for Cellular Based-UAVs
Yongchao Dang, Chafika Benzaïd, Yulong Shen, Tarik Taleb
IEEE GLOBECOM, 2020 · Best Paper Award
paperpdf

Derives an adaptive trustable residence area from serving-cell measurements and flags any reported GPS position that falls outside it.

Research Projects

  • AURORA-6G (2026 – present). AI-driven unified O-RAN for real-time autonomous zero-touch networks.
  • Cloudify-6G (Business Finland, 2023 – 2026). Radio protocol design and platform development on IS-Wireless O-RAN, OAI FlexRIC and srsRAN RIC; near-real-time KPM and xApps for RAN slicing and resource scheduling.
  • Drolo-I / Drolo-II (EU Recovery and Resilience Facility, 2023 – 2025). Spoofing-resistant positioning and network measurements of cellular-connected UAVs.
  • INSPIRE-5Gplus (EU Horizon 2020, 2019 – 2022). UAV position monitoring and anti-GPS-spoofing enabler; deliverables D2.1 and D3.1 on 5G security.
  • 5G!Drones (EU Horizon 2020, 2019 – 2022). Designed and developed a 5G-connected UAV system on Aalto's 5G network.
